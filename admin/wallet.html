Charges collected

Every withdrawal's service charge and every P2P transfer's company charge is logged here — this is the company's own earnings from fees, separate from package revenue. "Reversed" means an admin later rejected that withdrawal, so the charge was never actually kept. "Referral forfeited" is direct/retopup/level income that would have gone to a sponsor or upline who isn't active (no package bought yet, or blocked) — it was never credited to anyone, so it stays with the company.
