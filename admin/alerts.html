Suspicious activity & data errors

What is checked

Data errors (the numbers cannot be right)
  • Income or purchase wallet different from its history
  • Negative balance
  • Income above the 2X / 5X cap
  • ROI credited twice in a day, or more ROI days than allowed
  • Withdrawal waiting without approved KYC
Suspicious behaviour
  • 3+ members on one public IP address; one payout wallet used by several members
  • Repeated failed sign-ins or wrong transaction PINs in an hour
  • Deposit that does not match the blockchain
  • Very large pending withdrawal; blocked member with a pending withdrawal
  • Two members sending money back and forth; many sign-ups under one sponsor in a day

The system checks every 10 minutes by itself and shows a popup here when something new appears. Click a member's name in any alert to see their team, level income, money and activity.
