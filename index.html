Frequently Asked Questions

You can join from $5 with a sponsor's code, and every joining or retopup amount is in multiples of $5 (5, 10, 15, 20…). All amounts are in US dollars. Each email address and mobile number can be used for one account only.

5% of each package is credited on every trading day, Monday to Friday. Saturday and Sunday are non-trading days, so no ROI is credited on weekends.

You earn a share of every investment made by your team: level 1 2%, level 2 1%, and 0.2% for each of levels 3 to 12. For example, 100 people on your first level investing $5 each ($500) pay you $10. Level 1 unlocks with 1 active direct referral, level 2 with 2, and so on — you need 12 direct referrals to earn from all 12 levels. You can refer any number of people and earn 5% on each of them.
